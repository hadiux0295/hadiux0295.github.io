How to play

  1. Print one card per player (pages 2–7) and cut out the 48 calling pictures on page 1. Drop them in a hat or a stocking.
  2. Pick a pattern — one line is quickest. Draw one picture at a time, read it out and put it aside.
  3. Players cover that picture if it is on their card (candies, buttons or coins work as markers). The centre star is free.
  4. First to finish the pattern shouts “BINGO!” — check their pictures against the drawn pile.

Tip: slip the cards into sheet protectors and use a dry-erase marker to play again and again.
