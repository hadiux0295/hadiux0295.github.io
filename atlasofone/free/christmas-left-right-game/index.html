How to play

  1. One prize: everyone sits in a circle and one person holds a wrapped prize. Read the story; every time you hear LEFT or RIGHT, the prize moves that way. Whoever holds it at the end keeps it.
  2. Gift exchange: everyone holds their own wrapped gift and passes at the same time. When the story ends, you keep the gift in your hands.
  3. Read slowly and pause on every coloured word. Over 12 players? Make two circles with two prizes.

The story: Santa's Wobbly Sleigh

← LEFT = pass leftRIGHT → = pass right

It was Christmas Eve at the North Pole, and Santa was RIGHT on time — or so he thought. He climbed into his sleigh, looked LEFT, looked RIGHT, and shouted, "Ready, reindeer?"

Dasher stood on the LEFT. Dancer stood on the RIGHT. And little Comet stood RIGHT in the middle, chewing a candy cane he was not supposed to have.

"Off we go!" cried Santa. The sleigh leaned LEFT. Then it leaned RIGHT. Then it leaned so far LEFT that a big red present slid RIGHT off the back!

"Stop!" called Mrs. Claus, running out in her slippers. She picked up the present, turned RIGHT, then turned LEFT, and could not remember which way the sleigh had gone.

An elf pointed RIGHT. Another elf pointed LEFT. A third elf pointed straight up, because that is where flying sleighs usually go.

Meanwhile, high above the clouds, Santa checked his list. "Hmm," he said, "one present is LEFT out." He pulled hard to the RIGHT, swooped LEFT around a snowy mountain, and zoomed RIGHT back home.

Mrs. Claus was waiting RIGHT by the barn door with the present in her arms. "You LEFT this behind," she said, smiling.

"You are RIGHT, as always," laughed Santa. He tucked the present on the LEFT side of the sleigh, then moved it to the RIGHT side, just to be safe.

This time the sleigh did not wobble LEFT or RIGHT. It flew straight and smooth all night long, and every present arrived RIGHT where it belonged.

And the present that was LEFT behind? It is RIGHT in your hands. Merry Christmas — it's yours to keep!

The rhyme: Red, Green & Gold

Read a line, then pass the prize to whoever fits. Nobody fits? Pass to the left.

  1. Christmas is here, so let's begin —
    pass the gift to the person with the biggest grin.
  2. Red is the color of Santa's suit —
    pass to someone in red, from hat to boot.
  3. Green is the color of every tree —
    pass to someone in green, as quick as can be.
  4. Gold is the star that shines so bright —
    pass to someone wearing jewelry tonight.
  5. Some wear socks that are bright and loud —
    pass to the silliest socks in the crowd.
  6. Who has a pet with fur or fins?
    Pass it to them and see who grins.
  7. Who has flown in a plane this year?
    Pass to the traveler sitting near.
  8. Who likes cocoa more than tea?
    Pass to the cocoa fan — one, two, three!
  9. Now count two people to your right —
    pass it there and hold on tight!
  10. Who can whistle a jingly tune?
    Pass to a whistler — very soon!
  11. Who loves baking cookies and pie?
    Pass to a baker — don't be shy!
  12. Our Christmas game has reached the end —
    keep the gift, my lucky friend!
