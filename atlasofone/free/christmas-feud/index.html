How to play

  1. Face-off. One player from each team steps up. The host reads the question; the first to slap the table answers. The higher answer on the card wins control.
  2. Play or pass. The winning team plays the board or passes it to the other team.
  3. Guess the board. Players take turns naming answers. Each answer on the card scores its points.
  4. Three strikes. Three wrong answers and the other team gets one chance to steal all the round's points.
  5. Win. Most points after 8 boards wins.

The points show how common we think each answer is — not a survey. Close matches count: the host has the final say.
