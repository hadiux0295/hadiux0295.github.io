How to set it up

  1. Print pages 2 and 3 (card stock works best) and cut out the clue cards and the START and FINISH tags.
  2. Follow the trail on page 1: each clue points to a spot, and the next clue is hidden at that spot. The FINISH card — or a small present — goes at the last spot.
  3. Hand the kids the START card and clue No. 1. Read the rhymes aloud for little ones.

The 6 clues

Here is the whole trail — tap Where is it? to see each hiding spot.

  1. I'm tall and green and wear a star,
    my twinkly lights shine near and far.
    Look low beneath my branches wide —
    your next clue is tucked inside!

    Where is it?

    Christmas tree

  2. Where the family sits to watch a show,
    I've got cushions in a row.
    Lift them up and dig around —
    your next clue is waiting to be found!

    Where is it?

    Sofa cushions

  3. I'm hung up high with extra care,
    on Christmas Eve I'm waiting there.
    Reach inside me, top to toe —
    your clue is in there, off you go!

    Where is it?

    Christmas stocking

  4. I hum all night, I'm cold inside,
    where milk and juice and leftovers hide.
    Peek on my shelves and on my door —
    a chilly clue is there in store!

    Where is it?

    Refrigerator

  5. At night you rest your sleepy head
    on something soft upon your bed.
    Lift it up and you will see
    the next clue waiting patiently!

    Where is it?

    Pillow

  6. Once upon a time, it's true,
    I held the tales that were read to you.
    Search my shelves from end to end —
    a clue is hiding, clever friend!

    Where is it?

    Bookshelf
