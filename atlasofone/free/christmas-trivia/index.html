How to play

  1. Print one quiz sheet per player or team and set a timer — about 10 minutes.
  2. Read each question and the three answers aloud; players circle A, B or C.
  3. Swap sheets, read out the answers from the key and share the fun fact after each one. One point per right answer.

The 15 questions

Prefer to play from a phone? Here is the whole quiz — tap Answer to reveal each one.

  1. The legend of Santa Claus grew from stories about which saint?

    • ASt. Patrick
    • BSt. George
    • CSt. Nicholas
    Answer

    C. St. Nicholas — St. Nicholas was famous for secret gift-giving.

  2. St. Nicholas was a bishop in Myra, a town in which modern-day country?

    • ATurkey
    • BNorway
    • CGermany
    Answer

    A. Turkey — Myra is near today's town of Demre in Turkey.

  3. Which 1823 poem gave us the names of Santa's reindeer?

    • AJingle Bells
    • BA Visit from St. Nicholas
    • CThe Snow Queen
    Answer

    B. A Visit from St. Nicholas — It is also known by its first line, 'Twas the Night Before Christmas'.

  4. How many reindeer pull Santa's sleigh in the 1823 poem?

    • ATwelve
    • BSix
    • CEight
    Answer

    C. Eight — The poem names eight tiny reindeer.

  5. Which of these is one of the reindeer named in the 1823 poem?

    • ASparkle
    • BPrancer
    • CSnowball
    Answer

    B. Prancer — The others include Dasher, Dancer, Vixen, Comet and Cupid.

  6. In real life, which reindeer grow antlers?

    • AOnly males
    • BBoth males and females
    • COnly the strongest adult males
    Answer

    B. Both males and females — Reindeer are the only deer where females grow antlers too.

  7. In North America, wild reindeer are usually called what?

    • ACaribou
    • BElk
    • CMoose
    Answer

    A. Caribou — Caribou and reindeer are the same species.

  8. What is Santa traditionally called in Britain?

    • AThe Yule Gentleman
    • BOld Nick
    • CFather Christmas
    Answer

    C. Father Christmas — Father Christmas was a separate English figure who merged with Santa.

  9. What is Santa called in France?

    • ASinterklaas
    • BBabbo Natale
    • CPère Noël
    Answer

    C. Père Noël — Babbo Natale is his Italian name.

  10. Which cartoonist drew famous pictures of Santa for Harper's Weekly in the 1860s?

    • ANorman Rockwell
    • BThomas Nast
    • CWalt Kelly
    Answer

    B. Thomas Nast — Nast's drawings helped create Santa's jolly modern look.

  11. Which North American air-defence command 'tracks' Santa every Christmas Eve?

    • ANORAD
    • BNASA
    • CThe FBI
    Answer

    A. NORAD — It began with an air-defence command in 1955, when children's calls meant for Santa reached it by mistake.

  12. In Canada, letters to Santa can use which special postal code?

    • AH0H 0H0
    • BN0R 7H3
    • CX1M 4S5
    Answer

    A. H0H 0H0 — H0H 0H0 reads like 'Ho ho ho'.

  13. Which town in Finland calls itself the 'official hometown of Santa Claus'?

    • AHelsinki
    • BTurku
    • CRovaniemi
    Answer

    C. Rovaniemi — Its Santa Claus Village sits right on the Arctic Circle.

  14. In Swedish folklore, what is the small bearded Christmas gnome called?

    • AKobold
    • BTomte
    • CTroll
    Answer

    B. Tomte — In Norway and Denmark he is called a nisse.

  15. In the 1823 poem, what does St. Nick go up the chimney with after filling the stockings?

    • AA nod
    • BA wave
    • CA song
    Answer

    A. A nod — The poem says that, giving a nod, up the chimney he rose.
