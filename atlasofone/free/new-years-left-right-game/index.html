How to play

  1. One prize: everyone sits in a circle and one person holds a wrapped prize. Read the story; every time you hear LEFT or RIGHT, the prize moves that way. Whoever holds it at the end keeps it.
  2. Gift exchange: everyone holds their own wrapped gift and passes at the same time. Every story in the game moves each gift exactly one seat from where it started, so nobody goes home with the gift they brought — whatever the size of your group.
  3. Read slowly and pause on every coloured word. Over 12 players? Make two circles with two prizes.

The story: The Balloon Who Couldn't Wait

← LEFT = pass leftRIGHT → = pass right

In a big net on the ceiling, one hundred balloons were waiting for the countdown. Ninety-nine of them were very good at waiting. The one on the far LEFT was not. His name was Bo.

"Is it time yet?" asked Bo. "No," said the balloon on his RIGHT. "Is it time NOW?" "No," said the balloon on his LEFT. Bo wiggled LEFT. Bo jiggled RIGHT. Bo squeaked.

And then — pop! — out of the net he slipped. He floated LEFT over the snack table. He floated RIGHT over the dance floor. "It's time!" shouted Bo. "It is not," said everybody. A whole room of people looked LEFT, looked RIGHT, and looked up.

A little girl named June jumped to catch him. Bo bobbed LEFT. Her big brother jumped. Bo bobbed RIGHT. The dog jumped too, and Bo went RIGHT up to the ceiling fan, which is a bad place for a balloon.

"Help," said Bo, very quietly. June climbed onto a chair. She reached LEFT. She reached RIGHT. She caught his string with one finger, and she did not let go.

"Waiting is hard," said June. "It is the hardest thing there is," said Bo. So they waited together, RIGHT by the window, with nothing LEFT to do but watch the clock.

Ten, nine, eight… the net opened, and ninety-nine balloons came tumbling down — LEFT, and RIGHT, and LEFT again. But Bo was already exactly where he wanted to be.

And the prize? It waited the whole story, very patiently. Now it's yours to keep. Happy New Year!

The rhyme: Kids' Countdown

Read a line, then pass the prize to whoever fits. Nobody fits? Pass to the left.

  1. Ten little seconds, waiting in line —
    pass to a kid who is under nine.
  2. Nine o'clock bedtime? Not tonight!
    Pass to the sleepiest one in sight.
  3. Eight loud horns go toot, toot, toot —
    pass to whoever can whistle or hoot.
  4. Seven balloons float up to the sky —
    pass to the one who can jump so high.
  5. Six party hats, all pointy and tall —
    pass to the shortest one of all.
  6. Five big cheers for the year that's done —
    pass to whoever's the loudest one.
  7. Four sleepy yawns — who's yawning now?
    Pass to a yawner and take a bow.
  8. Three funny faces — show us, please!
    Pass to the silliest face you see.
  9. Two seats over, to your right —
    pass it on with all your might.
  10. One! It's here — give a great big cheer,
    and keep the prize. Happy New Year!
