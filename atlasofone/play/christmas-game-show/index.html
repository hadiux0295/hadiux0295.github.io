How to run the show

Set upOpen this page on the TV's browser, or plug a laptop or tablet into the TV (HDMI or screen mirroring). Press F for full screen. Type your team names on the home screen.
Christmas FeudPick a board. Ask the question. Teams guess the most common answers; tap a slot (or press its number 1–8) to reveal an answer and its points. Wrong guess = X strike. Three strikes and the other team can steal with one guess. Give the pot to a team with A or B. ×2 / ×3 doubles or triples the board.
Speed RoundTwo players from the winning team. Player 1 answers 5 quick questions in 20 seconds; tap the matching answers. Player 2 repeats with the same questions (duplicate answers are not allowed — ask for another). 200 points together wins the grand prize.
Christmas TriviaPick a quiz. Teams shout or write A, B or C. Enter reveals the answer and a fun fact; A/B gives 10 points. T starts a 20-second timer.
Keys1–8 reveal · X strike · A B award · Enter reveal all / answer · N next · R random board · T timer · F full screen · M mute · ? this help · Esc home
